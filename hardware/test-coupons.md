# Mechanical coupon set before full printing

Create one 8–10 mm thick coupon containing:

1. Through holes: 2.0, 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4 and 2.5 mm.
2. M2 heat-set insert bosses: manufacturer nominal plus +0.10, +0.20 and +0.30 mm.
3. Counterbores: 4.0, 4.2 and 4.4 mm, depths 1.5 and 2.0 mm.
4. Bearing seats:
   - OD 22 mm bearing: 21.8, 21.9, 22.0, 22.1 and 22.2 mm.
   - OD 15 mm bearing: 14.8, 14.9, 15.0, 15.1 and 15.2 mm.
5. Two 1.5 mm cable channels with 3, 4 and 5 mm bend radii.

Record printer, material, nozzle, layer height, wall count, orientation, measured diameter and fit result. Freeze compensation only for that printer/material/process combination.
