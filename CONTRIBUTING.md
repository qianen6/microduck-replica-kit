# Contributing

Contributions are most useful when they convert an inference into a measurement.

Please include:

1. hardware revision and exact part number;
2. test setup, command and raw measurement;
3. expected versus observed result;
4. photos or logs when they clarify fit or behavior;
5. the source URL and access date for externally sourced specifications.

Keep generated geometry out of pull requests unless its upstream license and attribution are included. Do not vendor whole upstream repositories; update `source-lock.json` and the fetch script instead.
